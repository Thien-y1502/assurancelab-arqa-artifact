# ARQA literature boundary

Closest work includes SANA collective attestation, HAGAR aggregate attestation,
Pontis heterogeneous-TEE interoperability, TrustMee self-verifying evidence,
IETF Distributed Remote Attestation, and multi-source evidence-fusion models.
These works cover transport, attestation aggregation, provenance, or trust
scoring. ARQA is restricted to business-level PASS/UNKNOWN/CONFLICT safety over
authenticated evidence and must not be presented as a new TEE protocol.

Primary links: [SANA](https://research.ibm.com/publications/sana-secure-and-scalable-aggregate-network-attestation), [HAGAR](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637543.3654655), [Pontis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306457326000440), [TrustMee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13148), [IETF DRA](https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-wang-rats-distributed-remote-attestation-02.html), [multi-source evidence fusion](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ress.2025.111842).

The v1/v2 local results explicitly expose the domain-majority collision instead
of hiding it.
